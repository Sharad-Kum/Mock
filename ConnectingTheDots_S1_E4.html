Flow Beats Title

GTM defines the terrain. Marketing turns it into demand and shapes the customer journey. Sales converts that demand into revenue.

Edition 3 was about clarity before ambiguity. This one tests it in sequencing GTM, Marketing and Sales.

One loose end, and the entire string is loosened.

A sequence — not a hierarchy, not a rotation of ownership. Overlap is integration, not interchange.

GTM starts the flow — and the flow talks back.

Where the flow talks back
Get the terrain wrong, and everything downstream drifts.
Get something downstream wrong, and GTM needs a second look.
When a function stalls, look for the seam no one owned.
Two moves that removed a handoff
Axonius named Joe Diamond president last year (now CEO) to run one GTM organization across Sales, Customer Success and Marketing.
6sense moved Latané Conant from CMO to CRO in 2023, putting every revenue function under one leader.

Neither move consolidated power. Both removed a handoff.

If GTM, Marketing and Sales answer “who is this for” with different names, that's three companies sharing a logo.

Org chart doesn't hold the flow together — shared artifacts do: one definition, one logic, one narrative, revisited on one cadence.

Different calendars are fine. Different rhythms are not.

Structures will keep shifting between integrated and peer-led. The flow stays constant; what won't survive it is anyone still asking which function should lead.
